CLICKER CO · PLAYER GUIDE

How To Play the Game

Pick a companion. Collect apples. Build your night shift.

01

Click your companion

Click or tap the animal picture to earn apple-shaped TOCKENS. Each click starts at 1 TOCKEN and gives 1 XP. Buy Click power upgrades in your empire to increase manual payouts; your rhythm bonus multiplies that upgraded value. You can also focus the animal and press Space or Enter.

02

Find your rhythm

Your clicks over the last second determine your bonus. It falls as you slow down and never exceeds 2×.

03

Open your empire

Press the castle icon on the side of the game to open Crew boosts, Night shift and Milestones. Close the panel with × or Escape to return to your companion.

Sidekicks earn 1 TOCKEN/s and bakers earn 10. Couriers make 50 automatic clicks/s; orchestras make 100. Each automatic click earns one base TOCKEN. Crew boosts and level bonuses improve this automatic output.

Automatic clicks never increase your manual click-speed multiplier, XP, or head-pat count. Every item’s price grows by 15% after each purchase of that item, rounded up.

04

Upgrade your crew

Each Velvet Paws purchase adds 1% output; Moonbeam Collar adds 5%; Midnight Treats adds 7.5%; Crown of the Cosmos adds 10%.

Starting prices: 25 / 50 / 100 / 200 TOCKENS. Each boost’s price increases by 15% per purchase, rounded up. Crew boosts add together and only affect crew earnings.

The separate Click power tab offers Click spark (+10%), Click surge (+25%), and Click nova (+50%) per purchase, starting at 100 / 500 / 2,500 TOCKENS. These add together, then multiply manual payouts by your rhythm bonus. Automatic crew clicks keep their 1-TOCKEN base.

05

Take the long way up

Manual head pats level up your friendship. Each level gives a separate TOCKEN gift and +5% crew production. Work toward 1,000 increasingly demanding milestones, reaching 10 million head pats, level 500 and one quadrillion lifetime TOCKENS. Browse 25 at a time or choose Ready gifts to find rewards you can claim.

When a milestone is ready, click Claim gift once. Each milestone can be claimed only once.

06

Make it yours. Keep it safe.

Choose a dog, cat, panda or monkey in Settings. The weekly item shop offers three cosmetic skins for TOCKENS and refreshes Mondays at 00:00 UTC. Purchased skins stay in Settings permanently. Mark, Pavly, John and Emmanuel remain invite-only and locked in local profiles. TOCKENS always stay red apples. Sound and reduced motion are optional. Choose Full Screen in Settings when your browser supports it.

Enter your name to save progress and purchased skins in this browser on this device. Return with the same name to continue. No password or server is required. Clearing site data removes local saves; use Export save for a backup. Your crew earns for up to 5 hours away. Guests start fresh and do not save progress. You can import a progress backup on another device; skin ownership stays with the local profile.
